Privacy Notice

Last updated 27 July 2026

Classical Wavelength is a non-commercial experimental service. This notice explains what happens to information you provide when you use it. It is deliberately short, because very little happens.

Who is responsible

This service is operated as a data controller under UK data protection law. You can contact the operator using the feedback form on the service.

What is collected, and what happens to it

What When Where it goes How long it is kept
What you type into the search box Every search Sent to Anthropic, which generates the recommendations. Recorded in the service's own logs only as a character count, never as text Anthropic retains data according to their own policy. The service keeps no copy
Your feedback, if you send it Only if you use the feedback form Stored in a Google Form belonging to the service operator Kept until the project ends, then deleted
Your email address Only if you choose to enter it in the feedback form Same Google Form Same. You never have to give it
Your browser and device type Only if you send feedback Same Google Form, to help diagnose display problems Same

What is NOT collected

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No cookies. The service does not set any cookies.
  • No analytics. Visits are not tracked or counted.
  • No advertising, and no advertising networks. The two links in the sidebar are ordinary links to organisations with no relationship to this service, and they are not paid.
  • Nothing is stored in your browser.
  • Your IP address is used momentarily to limit how many searches one person can run in an hour, and is not written to any permanent record.

Why this is lawful

Searches are processed because you asked for a recommendation — that is the performance of the service you requested. Feedback is processed with your consent, given when you choose to submit the form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by asking for your feedback to be deleted.

Third parties

Who What they receive Why
Anthropic The text you type into the search box They generate the recommendations. They do not receive your name, email or IP address
Google Your feedback, if you send it The feedback form is a Google Form
Railway Standard web server information They host the site

Each of these organisations has its own privacy policy. Some process data outside the United Kingdom.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask for a copy of any information held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to it being used. Because so little is collected, in practice this usually means asking for your feedback to be deleted, which will be done promptly.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

This is a test version shared with adults only. It is not intended for children, and no attempt is made to collect information from them.

This is a test version

Classical Wavelength is an experiment, shared with a small number of people. It may change or stop working at any time, and it may be withdrawn entirely. If that happens, the feedback collected will be deleted.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it.
